# Promo Video Storyboard

## Goal

Show that Net Infra SaaS helps telecom and infrastructure teams move from scattered field information to a structured operational workspace.

## Recommended Duration

30 to 60 seconds

## Structure

### Scene 1. The problem

Visual:

- fast cuts of chats, spreadsheets, paper notes, and disconnected maps

Voiceover or captions:

- Infrastructure work slows down when information is scattered

### Scene 2. Shared workspace

Visual:

- opening Net Infra SaaS on a phone or tablet
- entering the main workspace

Voiceover or captions:

- One workspace for network infrastructure, field updates, and active tasks

### Scene 3. Infrastructure visibility

Visual:

- infrastructure map
- cabinets
- fiber routes
- task-linked context

Voiceover or captions:

- Keep routes, cabinets, and field changes visible in one place

### Scene 4. Field operations

Visual:

- muff notebook
- cabinet view
- active task badge

Voiceover or captions:

- Help field teams document changes while office teams stay aligned

### Scene 5. Team coordination

Visual:

- switching between tasks and infrastructure records
- visible active work and structured records

Voiceover or captions:

- Improve accountability, reduce undocumented changes, and onboard new employees faster

### Scene 6. Closing

Visual:

- strong product overview frame
- app logo or title

Voiceover or captions:

- Net Infra SaaS. Infrastructure operations, documented and coordinated.

## Recording Notes

- Use real product screens when possible
- Prefer smooth touch interactions and short transitions
- Keep text overlays large and simple
- Focus on clarity, not cinematic effects
