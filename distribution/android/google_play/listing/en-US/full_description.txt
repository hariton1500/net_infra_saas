Net Infra SaaS is a workspace for telecom teams and network infrastructure operations specialists.

The app helps companies manage tasks, network cabinets, splice closures, fiber routes, field changes, and employee coordination in one shared system.

With Net Infra SaaS, teams can:

- create and track operational tasks
- document network cabinets and infrastructure nodes
- manage splice closures, cables, ports, and fiber connections
- view infrastructure objects and routes on a map
- link infrastructure changes to active tasks
- keep office teams and field employees synchronized
- store technical records inside the company workspace

Net Infra SaaS is designed for internet service providers, contractors, engineers, and infrastructure teams that need a specialized tool for operating and maintaining network infrastructure.
