# Promo Video Voice-over Script

This file contains ready-to-use voice-over templates for the first product promo video.

## English

### 30-second version

Infrastructure teams lose time when data is scattered across chats, spreadsheets, paper notes, and disconnected maps.

Net Infra SaaS brings routes, cabinets, field changes, and active tasks into one shared operational workspace.

Document infrastructure faster, keep office and field teams aligned, and reduce the risk of undocumented changes.

Net Infra SaaS. Infrastructure operations, documented and coordinated.

### 45-second version

When infrastructure knowledge is scattered, teams slow down. Important field changes get lost, work is duplicated, and coordination becomes harder than it should be.

Net Infra SaaS gives telecom and network operations teams one shared workspace for cabinets, fiber routes, field updates, and active tasks.

It helps office staff and field crews work from the same operational picture, with clearer records, better accountability, and easier onboarding for growing teams.

Net Infra SaaS. Built for real infrastructure operations.

### Caption-friendly short lines

- One workspace for network infrastructure
- Keep tasks and field updates connected
- Give every team member better visibility
- Reduce undocumented infrastructure changes
- Built for real telecom operations

Это помогает всем участникам работать в одном операционном контексте, держать записи в порядке, усиливать ответственность и быстрее подключать новых сотрудников к работе.
