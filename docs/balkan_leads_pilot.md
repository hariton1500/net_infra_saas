## Segmented Message Angles

### For Local ISPs

Pain: network data and field support scattered across Excel, maps, chats, and technician memory.

Offer: a simple SaaS to keep fiber lines, cabinets, closures, customers, outages, tickets, and technician reports in one place.

Pilot: one town, one district, or one network segment in 7-14 days.

### For FTTH Contractors

Pain: project progress, photo reports, crews, client updates, and as-built documentation are hard to keep synchronized.

Offer: a field-to-office workflow for FTTH construction: tasks, objects, photos, status, and exportable documentation.

Pilot: one active FTTH build or one recent completed project reconstructed in the system.

### For Engineering / Design Firms

Pain: gap between design, field changes, and final as-built documentation.

Offer: collaboration layer between design, field crews, contractors, and operator handover.

Pilot: one FTTH design package carried through field verification and as-built status.
