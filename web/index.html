Frequently asked questions

Clear answers help future customers and search engines understand what the product is for.

How is Net Infra SaaS different from a generic task manager?

A generic task manager stores tasks. Net Infra SaaS connects tasks with network assets, fiber routes, splice points, cabinets, and infrastructure documentation.

Is the service useful for a small technical team?

Yes. A small team can start with a shared operational base and expand the workflow as the company grows.

What kind of data can be managed?

The service is designed for network cabinets, splice points, cable lines, routes, employees, tasks, and changes related to network operations.

Can it support field operations?

Yes. Net Infra SaaS helps connect office planning, assigned field work, and actual infrastructure changes made on site.
